HOW TO PLAY

  • Blue starts bottom-left and must reach the top-left exit. Pink starts top-right and must reach the bottom-right exit.
  • Move one tile up, down, left or right. No diagonal moves. You cannot step onto the other player.
  • Collect 3 crystals (◆ = 1, ★ bonus = 2), then stand on YOUR exit to win.
  • On your turn you can Move or Place a Wall. Each player starts with 6 walls.
  • Walls can never block a player completely. Invalid walls are refused.
  • ◈ Shield: lets you break one wall by moving into it.
  • ◉ Scan: reveals your opponent's distance to their exit and crystal count.
  • Match lasts 80 turns. If nobody wins, the most crystals wins. Equal crystals = draw.
